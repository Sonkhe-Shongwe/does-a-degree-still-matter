# Data source feasibility register — 26 October 2026

**Status:** official sources and documentation identified; no raw microdata or survey-variable audit completed.

| Source | Intended use | Present evidence | Outstanding validation |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Stats SA 2026 QLFS](https://isibaloweb.statssa.gov.za/pages/surveys/pss/qlfs/2026/qlfs2026.php) | **Primary:** education and employment comparisons | Source-level survey releases and documentation for demographic, educational and labour status concepts | Actual files; values and eligibility codes; weights, strata/PSU; missingness; subgroup counts; periods and licensing |
| [Stats SA Labour Market Dynamics](https://isibaloweb.statssa.gov.za/pages/surveys/pss/lmd/lmdp.php) | **Conditional:** earnings | Official publication series documented | Completeness, imputation, inflation adjustment, comparability and availability of valid uncertainty estimates |
| [NIDS via SALDRU](https://saldru.uct.ac.za/nids/data-access) | Optional historical longitudinal context | Historical 2008–2017 survey waves documented | Registration/terms, relevant indicators and historical framing; not evidence for 2026 outcomes |

## Primary defensible analysis
Survey-weighted employment, unemployment (labour-force denominator) and labour-force participation by correctly coded education groups, with age/province comparisons where effective samples permit.

## Not yet supported
- Causal impact of earning a qualification.
- Typical time between graduation and first job.
- Reliable earnings premiums without a separate earnings quality audit.
- Fine-grained field-of-study rankings without enough data.

## Go/no-go checks
Verify legitimate access, variable definitions, survey design fields, cross-period comparability, unweighted sample counts, disclosure constraints and reproducibility procedures. Keep income analysis optional until proven feasible.
