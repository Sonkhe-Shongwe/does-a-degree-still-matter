# Does a Degree Still Matter in 2026?

> **Status: Planning / dataset validation — no analysis or findings claimed.**

## Business question
How do education levels relate to employment and earnings outcomes in South Africa, and how do these associations differ across population groups?

## Intended outcome
A reproducible, employer-facing analytical investigation with documented data provenance, SQL/Python analysis, Power BI reporting and evidence-based recommendations. No large standalone app is planned.

## Data availability
Primary candidate: Statistics South Africa QLFS microdata. Earnings candidate: Labour Market Dynamics. Optional longitudinal context: NIDS. Confirm survey weights, variable definitions, year compatibility and reuse conditions.

## Skills to demonstrate
Survey-aware statistics, SQL, Python, Power BI, labour-market indicators, regression and uncertainty.

## Planned workflow
1. Verify dataset access, licensing, coverage and fields.
2. Define measurable research/business questions and success criteria.
3. Audit and prepare data; document assumptions and quality checks.
4. Build and validate analytical models and methods.
5. Produce an interpretable Power BI report and written findings.
6. Publish reproducibility instructions and limitations.

## Repository structure
- `data/`: acquisition instructions, never unlicensed or sensitive source data.
- `sql/`: analytical queries.
- `src/`: reproducible Python scripts.
- `notebooks/`: exploratory work.
- `powerbi/`: Power BI files and screenshots when ready.
- `docs/`: data sources, methodology, model design and findings.
- `reports/`: final analytical reports.
- `tests/`: validation checks.
- `assets/`: approved visuals.

**No results, datasets, or implementation have been published yet.**
